Rich Culture

Spain’s culture is composed of a mixture of traditions and passion that can be seen by their world famous flamenco dance and lively festivals. Flamenco is known for it’s music and hard foodwork as well as heartfelt expressions, apart from this there are festivals like la tomatina where thousands of people gather to participate in a massive tomato war.

Stunning Destinations

The country of Spain exhibits historic sites and architectural masterpieces across its territory. Visitors flock to Barcelona's sagrada familia because this Antonia Gaudi design masterpiece charms all who view it through its Gothic and Art Nouveau fusion of styles each year. The property exhibits the royal heritage of Spain through its 3,400 grand rooms that feature opulent chandeliers and grand frescoes together with priceless artifacts.

Delicious Food

Spanish cuisine unites flavorful elements with traditional ingredients along with age-old customs in its cooking practice. Tourists should experience the traditional saffron rice dish "Paella" in its unique Valencia version since it includes choices of seafood, chicken or rabbit. The traditional Spanish dining tradition of Tapas presents people with small and flavorful dishes that include patatas bravas with croquetas and gambas
